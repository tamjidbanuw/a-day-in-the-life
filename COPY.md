# A Day in the Life of the World — the complete copy

Every word in the story, in reading order. Rewrite the text under any heading,
hand the file back, and it goes into the right place in the page.

### How to edit this

- **Leave the `##` and `###` heading lines alone.** The `[anchor]` in each one is
  how the text is matched back to the page.
- **Keep every `{placeholder}` exactly as written** in Part 2. Those are filled in
  from the data while the page runs — a country name, a number of hours. Change
  the words around them freely; delete one and the page gets a hole.
- Blocks marked **TITLE** or **NAME** are headings, so short is better.
- A block shown as one paragraph here may be several bold runs in the page. The
  bolding is reapplied on the way back in; write plainly.

The four scores are an illustrative index built from the same sources used throughout: daily free-time share (OECD Time Use), life expectancy (Our World in Data), a community score combining social support, freedom and generosity (World Happiness Report), and international arrivals (World Bank).
