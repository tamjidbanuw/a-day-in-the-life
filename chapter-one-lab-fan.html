A day, and how much of it is argued about

Thirty-five countries keep a diary of the ordinary day. Two ways of looking at the same 1,440 minutes: on the left, how the day divides and how much countries disagree about each part; on the right, what a long working day costs in rest. Click a wedge to recolour the countries by it.

The Day, by Block

Angle is share of the day · radius is how much countries disagree

Source: OECD Time Use Database, 35 countries, minutes per day, both sexes. Radius shows each block’s full spread across the 35 as a percentage of its own average, so blocks of very different size can be compared for how much they move. “Other” is drawn dashed and off the scale: it is a residual category of 1% of the day that varies 215%, which would flatten every other wedge if it set the outer edge.

Work Against Rest

One bubble per country · size is GDP per person

Source: OECD Time Use Database; GDP per capita via data/adl-data.js. Bubble area is proportional to GDP per person, on a square-root scale so area rather than radius carries the value. The dashed line is the least-squares fit.
