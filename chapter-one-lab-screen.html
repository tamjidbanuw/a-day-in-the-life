an ordinary day

Thirty-five countries keep a time diary — a record of what thousands of people actually did across 24 hours. Every one of them is dividing the same 1,440 minutes.

The chart below pairs each country’s working day against what it has left. The red bar between the two dots is the distance between obligation and rest. Hover any row and the rest of the page follows it.
